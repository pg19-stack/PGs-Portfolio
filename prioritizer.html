          How the scoring works

          Priority score

          Each idea gets a score out of 100, built from four inputs:

          • Value counts for 40%
          • Feasibility counts for 25%
          • Time to impact counts for 20% (faster scores higher)
          • Risk counts for 15% (lower risk scores higher)

          Lane rules

          • Quick Win: live within 3 months, feasibility of 4 or more, value of 3 or more
          • Platform Bet: value of 4 or more, feasibility of 3 or more, live within 12 months
          • Frontier R&D: value of 4 or more, but hard to deliver or more than a year out
          • Revisit later: everything else

          The weights are a starting point. In a real portfolio they are agreed with the sponsor before any idea is scored. Your entries stay in this browser and are not sent anywhere.
